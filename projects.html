Predator-Prey Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

2026 · Reinforcement Learning · Multi-Agent Systems · NumPy

Built a predator-prey environment where both agents learn simultaneously through self-play, each acting on its own partially observable state (a POMDP) via an 8-ray lidar sensor rather than full knowledge of the maze. Implemented DQN with a target network and experience replay from scratch in NumPy.

Documented the debugging process alongside the math: a training collapse caused by the network chasing its own shifting target, a deterministic action loop from under-penalized wall collisions, and the non-stationarity that appears once both agents adapt at once.

Interactive Möbius Strip Visualizer

2026 · Three.js · WebGL · Interactive 3D

Built a 3D interactive tool exploring Möbius strip topology: cut the strip and watch the resulting pieces unfold in real time to reveal how they are (or aren't) linked. All geometry is generated procedurally from the surface's mathematical parametrization rather than approximated shapes.

Verified the underlying topology numerically (orientability, self-intersection, arc length) before relying on the visual output.
