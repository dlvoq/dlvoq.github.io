01 - THE OBJECT

The Möbius Strip

A band with a single 180° twist, joining what look like two separate faces into one. See what happens when you cut it.

MÖBIUS PARAMETRIZATION
x(u,v) = (R + v·cosu2)·cos u(1)
y(u,v) = (R + v·cosu2)·sin u(2)
z(u,v) = v·sinu2(3)
R Central radius - distance from the center of the ring to the strip's core curve (here, 2.2).
u Angle around the ring - runs from 0 to 360° (2π rad) once per full revolution.
v Position across the width - ranges from −0.7 to 0.7; 0 is the centerline.

u⁄2 is why the twist happens: u goes all the way around (360°), but u⁄2 only gets halfway (180°). So by the time you're back where you started, "top" and "bottom" have swapped - one side, one edge.
